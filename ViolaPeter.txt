C support
Peter is feeding the birds in the castle courtyard. Viola rushes in, happily greeting Peter, but that causes the birds to get startled and fly off. Peter advises Viola to keep her voice down to not scare the birds. She understands and quietly sits next to him. A few birds come back and Viola asks Peter why he likes birds so much. Peter says that he likes animals in general and enjoys their company. He finds himself more at peace with animals than people hence why he lives all by himself out in the mountains. Viola then asks if he doesnt like people and Peter replies that he doesnt mind them either way and enjoys the company of kind people like Viola.
